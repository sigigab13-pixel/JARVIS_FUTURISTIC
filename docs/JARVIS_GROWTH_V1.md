# JARVIS Futuristic — V1 Growth Plan

## Goal

Do not optimize for a huge audience before the product proves value.

The first growth target is:

**100 sign-ups → 20 active users → 5 strong repeat users or early paid candidates**

The strongest V1 acquisition hook is the Children's Content workflow: an idea can become a story, character assets, images, and a production-ready workflow.

## Core acquisition loop

```text
Social post / short video
        ↓
JARVIS landing / sign-in screen
        ↓
Create account
        ↓
Complete one useful task
        ↓
Save / share the result
        ↓
Friend or creator discovers JARVIS
        ↺
```

## First audiences

1. Children's-content creators
2. Teachers, parents, and educational creators
3. Small YouTube/TikTok/Facebook creators
4. AI builders and early adopters

Do not market every JARVIS capability at once. Lead with one visible outcome, then introduce the broader platform.

## Channel plan

### Facebook / Instagram

Post short demonstrations:
- Idea → children's story
- One character → a consistent story world
- JARVIS creates the next part of the workflow

Use the live product link and ask for testers, feedback, and shares.

### X

Post the engineering journey:
- production bugs found
- fixes
- new verified capabilities
- screenshots and short clips
- weekly milestones

The objective is credibility and early technical users.

### YouTube

Use JARVIS-generated children's-content demonstrations as both product examples and discovery content.

Do not claim fully autonomous production when the current adapter or provider has not been verified.

### Direct outreach

For the first 100 users, personal outreach is efficient. Invite people who already make educational or children's content to test one workflow and give feedback.

## Referral links

The public share button uses:

`https://jarvis-futureristic-679d.vercel.app/?ref=share`

The app captures the referral value locally and preserves it in email signup metadata for early attribution. This is intentionally lightweight; a central referral analytics table can be added after V1 proves demand.

## What to measure

The eventual growth dashboard should track:

- landing visits
- account creation
- first successful task
- Children's Factory starts
- completed drafts
- render attempts
- publish-ready outputs
- repeat users
- referral signups

Activation is more important than raw page views.

## V1 growth rule

Every new growth feature must answer one of these questions:

**Does it help someone discover JARVIS?**

**Does it help them understand JARVIS?**

**Does it help them reach a successful first result?**

**Does it help them share that result?**

Anything else stays out of the V1 growth lane.
